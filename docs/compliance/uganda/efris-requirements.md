## Conflicts noticed between sources
Vendor and blog pages variously say EFRIS applies to "all companies", to "VAT-registered only", or that adoption dates differ (2020 vs 2021). The **only** statements adopted above come from URA's own pages. The January 2021 mandatory date for VAT-registered taxpayers appears in several secondary sources but was **not** confirmed on a URA page I read.

## What this record does NOT establish
It does not say ASG ERP is permitted to submit to EFRIS, what its technical interface is, whether accreditation is needed, or that any behaviour is legally sufficient. Those require the EFRIS handbook/technical documentation, URA confirmation, and professional review.
